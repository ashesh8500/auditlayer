# AuditLayer Improvement Vision

## North star

AuditLayerMedia becomes the longitudinal brand-intelligence system for people and organizations that partner with us: individual creators, experts, small businesses, established brands, and enterprises. It turns changing evidence into an evolving strategic model, inspectable decisions, measurable experiments, and useful interfaces that improve over time.

The intelligence model is authoritative. Reports are immutable, meeting-ready accessibility projections of that intelligence for a particular audience, purpose, and point in time; they are not the canonical state themselves.

The mission is not to add generic SaaS surface area or generate more prose. It is to improve the composition:

**Partner → Workspace relationship → Subject → Living Brief → Channels and markets → Evidence → Claims and findings → Decisions → Experiments → Observed outcomes → Accessible projections**

Reports remain immutable, meeting-ready outputs. Continuity lives in validated, versioned records. Every recommendation should be traceable to evidence, a strategic lens, and an observed or explicitly unknown outcome.

## Improvement programs

### P1 — Evidence truth and provenance

Make every material metric, finding, comparison, and score walkable to evidence with source, observation time, freshness, confidence, and limitations. Missing evidence renders `Data needed`, never invented precision or a punitive zero.

### P2 — Longitudinal memory and learning

Make the Living Brief, evidence snapshots, report versions, decisions, accepted/rejected recommendations, and outcomes form a coherent history. The system should distinguish real change from changed methodology or context.

### P3 — Report intelligence and editorial quality

Increase the decision value of the six customer answers and the 15-section method without bloating the artifact. Recommendations must be specific, prioritized, measurable, scale-aware, and written in Narin-calibrated premium editorial language.

### P4 — Domain calibration and peer validity

Treat account type, niche, follower tier, platform role, offer model, and evidence quality as first-class. Peers must be real, same-tier by default, relation-aware, and justified. Domain calibration must be tested rather than asserted.

### P5 — Product experience and creator agency

Keep the customer path calm and short while making continuity visible: choose a subject, confirm channels and lens, watch a truthful three-state process, inspect the Living Brief, and act on the next decision. Avoid dashboard sprawl and AI-theater.

### P6 — Bounded runtime, economics, and reliability

Production report inference stays DeepSeek V4 Flash-only, typed, bounded, stateless, and tool-free. Deterministic Python owns retrieval, retries, validation, scoring, rendering, persistence, and cost/time limits. Improvements must reduce failure or cost without hiding degraded quality.

### P7 — Trust, privacy, and founder operability

Protect tenant boundaries, credentials, report artifacts, and commercial state. Every privileged mutation is typed, permission-checked, and auditable. Founders can understand and recover the system without raw SQL; customers never see internal traces or fabricated progress.

### P8 — Measurable creator and business outcomes

Close the loop from recommendation to experiment to observed result. AuditLayer should learn which recommendations produce useful creator outcomes while preserving causal humility. Product growth must come from demonstrated report value, not manipulative friction.

### P9 — Universal brand-intelligence ontology

Define a small, testable semantic model that works across individuals, creators, small businesses, brands, and enterprises without reducing every partner to a social handle. Model nouns, relationships, states, actions, constraints, policy, provenance, and time. Keep SQL and application contracts authoritative; semantic artifacts begin as versioned, drift-tested projections rather than new infrastructure.

### P10 — Coherent, accessible product experience

Treat UI consistency and intuitiveness as correctness. Shared tokens, typography, spacing, radii, color, interaction states, navigation, empty/loading/error states, and language should form one restrained system across marketing, authentication, product, reports, support, desktop, and mobile. Accessibility and responsive behavior are release gates, not polish deferred indefinitely.

### P11 — Commercial truth and offer learning

Connect product decisions to real partner needs, willingness to pay, delivery cost, and observed value. Pricing, plans, onboarding, sales promises, and entitlements are versioned hypotheses with evidence and explicit uncertainty. Never sell a distinction the runtime cannot deterministically provide; never let conversion work outrank trust.

### P12 — Production architecture, performance, and safe release

Make the existing Vercel, Supabase, and Hetzner system fast, portable, observable, recoverable, and easier to authenticate against. Bound interfaces so hosting may change without rewriting the domain model. Measure browser responsiveness, audit latency, database/network cost, failure modes, and recovery separately. Release only through preview evidence, rollback readiness, a bounded production canary, and post-deploy verification.

## Intelligent improvement behavior

The loop may assign zero cards. It should stop, observe, or ask for a missing capability when confidence, evidence, capacity, or release safety is inadequate. Work is valuable only when it leaves inspectable artifacts and changes what the system can truthfully know, decide, deliver, or verify.

## Composition test

An idea belongs in this mission only if it improves at least one program and names how it composes into the canonical flow. Small changes are welcome when they create a reusable contract, a falsifiable result, or a visibly better customer decision. Feature count is not progress.

## Anti-goals

- No production deploys, migrations, restarts, payment actions, customer-data mutations, or live report token spend from ordinary improvement cards. A separate release gate may promote already-verified work only with preflight evidence, rollback readiness, a bounded canary, and post-deploy verification.
- No generic dashboards, autonomous open-ended report agents, hidden provider fallbacks, model browsing, judge loops, or recursive production delegation.
- No rewrites that discard immutable reports, accepted decisions, customer history, or current architecture contracts.
- No claims of quality, calibration, reliability, or business value without a runnable check and honest null/failure outcome.
- No blind queue consumption, capability-substitution workarounds, infinite retries, or feature accumulation without system-level composition.
