# AuditLayer Improvement Fundamentals

These principles govern every autonomous improvement card and outrank local convenience.

1. **Truth before fluency.** Evidence-backed modesty is more valuable than confident prose. Every material claim names its evidence or its limitation.
2. **The creator is a subject, not a handle.** Channels are observations of a durable subject. Reports are outputs, not the identity model.
3. **Continuity is explicit and versioned.** Living Brief versions, evidence snapshots, methodology versions, report versions, decisions, and outcomes remain distinguishable and immutable where required.
4. **Observed facts and strategic perspective are separate.** Context may change priorities, thresholds, and recommendations; it may not silently change evidence.
5. **Reports are the paid artifact.** Product and infrastructure work must ultimately improve report truth, decision value, delivery, or durable continuity.
6. **Domain calibration beats generic analytics.** Account type, niche, scale, platform role, and relationship framing are verified before recommendations or peer comparisons.
7. **Creator dignity is a constraint.** Show strengths, uncertainty, and tractable next actions. Never demoralize with invalid aspirational comparisons or false precision.
8. **Agentic where useful, deterministic where authoritative.** Models analyze bounded typed context. Code owns permissions, money, state transitions, scoring rules, validation, persistence, retries, and release gates.
9. **Production inference is a separate system.** Audit report generation remains DeepSeek V4 Flash-only, bounded, stateless, tool-free, and without hidden fallback. The engineering worker profile may use auxiliary vision only to inspect development artifacts.
10. **Security and tenant isolation are product properties.** Browser clients never gain service-role behavior. Private artifacts use request-scoped access. Privileged actions are typed and audited.
11. **Falsifiability first.** Every idea declares a measurable check, baseline, sample/evidence source, and honest-null condition before implementation.
12. **No synthetic proof.** Fixtures may verify software contracts; they do not establish customer efficacy, model calibration, or business impact.
13. **Elegance is operational stability.** Prefer canonical modules, typed structures, explicit invariants, minimal duplicate loops, concise interfaces, and correction tips.
14. **Founder-operable, customer-calm.** Internal complexity should become clear recovery information for founders and restrained, truthful progress for customers.
15. **No production mutations from this loop.** Worktree code, docs, fixtures, and local tests only. Deployment and live migrations require an explicit independent release approval.

## Required card check

Every implementation card must state:

- **Object:** what typed entity, contract, or user decision is changing?
- **Claim:** what improvement is expected?
- **Evidence:** which real repository artifact, observed dataset, or fixture-backed software contract evaluates it?
- **Baseline:** what current behavior is it compared against?
- **Verdict rule:** what counts as verified, failed, partial, or parked?
- **Safety:** why the check cannot mutate production, spend live report tokens, or cross tenant boundaries?
