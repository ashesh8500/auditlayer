# Loved by Our Community Homepage Section

## Objective

Add an editorial social-proof section to the public homepage using the first approved community message from the Kas di Kos Team.

## Placement

After `Inside every report` and before Pricing. This lets the proof reinforce report value immediately before the purchase decision without interrupting the methodology explanation.

## Composition

- Dark-forest editorial introduction panel:
  - Heading: `Loved by our community.`
  - Supporting line explaining that the section contains words from teams using the work to make decisions.
- Warm report-like quote panel:
  - Large, readable blockquote broken into three paragraphs for rhythm.
  - Restrained accent rule and quote mark.
  - Attribution: `Kas di Kos Team`.

## Approved quote

> The information you provided is incredibly insightful and offers actionable steps that align with our direction.
>
> We fully agree with your recommendation to showcase products in real lived-in spaces that are attainable because we believe that curation exists at every level.
>
> Your analysis confirms that we are making the right decisions with this pivot, and we cannot thank you enough.

— Kas di Kos Team

## Rules

- Preserve the wording and meaning; normalize only line wrapping and paragraph breaks.
- Do not add an unsupported role, result, metric, account handle, or company claim.
- Do not turn the section into a carousel until multiple approved testimonials exist.
- Keep quotation text readable on desktop and 390px mobile.

## Acceptance

- Section is visible before Pricing.
- Semantic `blockquote` and `cite` markup are present.
- No horizontal overflow or clipping.
- Full web validation and desktop/mobile visual QA pass.
