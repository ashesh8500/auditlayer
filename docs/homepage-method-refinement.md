# Homepage Method Section Refinement

## Problem

The current homepage places a three-card trust strip immediately above a second three-card methodology section. The repeated pattern, abrupt light-to-dark transition, detached privacy claim, and mismatch between `diagnosis` in the stated sequence and `Calibrate` in the steps make the area feel fragmented.

## Direction

Consolidate the trust and methodology content into one restrained editorial section.

### Structure

1. Light panel rather than another full dark band.
2. One clear introduction:
   - Kicker: `The method`
   - Headline: `From signal to a decision you can use.`
   - Explanation distinguishing observation, interpretation, and prioritization.
3. Three stacked method rows:
   - Observe
   - Diagnose
   - Decide
4. One quiet operating-standards footer:
   - Private by default
   - Limits stay visible
   - Calibrated by media strategy

## Copy principles

- Avoid jargon such as `Calibrate` without explanation.
- Align step labels with the evidence → diagnosis → decision model.
- State evidence limits without duplicating the later FAQ/evidence section.
- Keep privacy as an operating standard, not a disconnected marketing card.
- Prefer direct, decision-ready language over generic analytics claims.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: introduction left, stacked method rows right, standards footer across the section.
- Mobile: introduction, method rows, then standards; no horizontal overflow or undersized text.

## Acceptance

- Remove the detached Product trust strip.
- Preserve truthful privacy, evidence-limit, and media-calibration claims.
- Improve visual transition from the hero preview to Method and from Method to the report contents section.
- Pass tests, typecheck, lint, build, browser smoke tests, and desktop/mobile visual QA.
