# AuditLayerMedia company context

AuditLayerMedia is a founder-operated, general-use brand and social media management and competitive-intelligence company serving individuals, creators, teams, businesses, established brands, and enterprises. Health, wellness, biohacking, and expert-led work are areas of founder expertise, not customer eligibility restrictions. Narin owns report quality, contextual calibration, product, sales, and marketing. Ashesh owns engineering, infrastructure, security, and production releases.

The production topology is intentionally small: Next.js on Vercel, Supabase for Auth/Postgres/Storage/Realtime, and two Python worker instances on the Hetzner VPS. Reports are private immutable HTML artifacts. The product worker uses DeepSeek V4 Flash only.
